wherever they go.

Most pet cameras have blind spots. Snugbi doesn't. The smart collar broadcasts a Bluetooth signal — Snugbi reads the distance and follows your pet from room to room, automatically.

📡
Collar signal tracking

Health monitoring

Not just watching.
Understanding.

Snugbi uses camera, microphone, motion sensors, and collar data to build a complete picture of your pet's health — every hour of every day. Week 1 is learning your pet's normal. Week 2 onwards, any deviation triggers an alert.

📷
Camera observation
Detects posture, movement, excessive grooming, hiding, limping, pacing, and body language. IR night vision means monitoring never stops.
Visual AI
🎙️
Vocalisation tracking
Logs every meow, bark, whine, purr, and growl. Tracks frequency and intensity. Unusual vocalisation — too much or sudden silence — is flagged immediately.
Audio AI
🏃
Activity tracking
Collar accelerometer tracks every movement. Builds a daily activity score. Decline over several days is one of the earliest illness signals there is.
Collar
😴
Sleep quality
Tracks sleep duration, depth, location, and restlessness. Unusual sleep patterns — different spots, restless nights — logged and included in the daily report.
Collar
🌡️
Temperature trend
Collar monitors body temperature continuously. Not absolute readings — trend monitoring. A sustained rise above your pet's individual baseline is flagged as a potential fever.
Trend
🚨
Anomaly detection
After learning your pet's baseline, Snugbi flags anything that deviates — lethargy, anxiety, excessive grooming, seizure-like movement, sudden behaviour change. You know before it becomes visible.

Sensor stack

Eight ways to
see everything.

A layered sensor system — camera, microphone, motion sensors, and collar data. If one has a limitation, the others fill the gap. No single point of failure.

📷
Camera (recessed)
Wide-angle, IR night vision. Follows your pet at close range. Detects posture, grooming, hiding, body language.
🎙️
Microphone
Sound classification AI. Logs all vocalisations regardless of camera angle or lighting conditions.
🏃
Collar accelerometer
Tracks every movement from your pet's body. Activity, posture, sleep quality, scratching, seizure detection.
🌡️
Collar temperature
Continuous body temperature trend monitoring. Flags sustained rises above your pet's individual baseline.
📡
Bluetooth RSSI (following)
Bot reads collar signal strength to calculate distance. Follows your pet from room to room automatically.
🔁
PIR motion sensor (360°)
Detects movement in all directions — even when the camera is blocked or the pet is right next to the bot.
👆
Proximity + touch sensor
Detects when your pet is too close for the camera. Activates cuddle mode when they sit on Snugbi.
⚡
Bot accelerometer
Detects if the bot has been knocked, pushed into a corner, or stuck. Self-reports its own limitations in the app.

Play features

Keeps them
happy and active.

A healthy pet is stimulated as well as monitored. Snugbi plays with your pet daily, adapts to their preferences, and keeps them physically and mentally active while you're away.

🔦
LED dot chase
Eye-safe LED projects a moving dot. Patterns vary automatically. AI learns which patterns your cat responds to.
🪶
Peek a boo
Feather arm appears and disappears rhythmically. Timing varies so cats can't predict it — mimics prey in grass.
🫣
Hide and seek
Snugbi moves to a hidden spot, makes a sound, and waits. Cat finds it. Time logged in activity score.
🌿
Scent mode
Releases catnip or valerian micro-amounts. Scientifically shown to reduce stress behaviours in cats by up to 30%.
🍖
Hide treat
Bot drops a treat in a spot and drives away. Cat hunts for it. Activates natural foraging behaviour.
🔊
Sound attraction
Bird calls, crinkle, chirping. AI learns which sounds get your cat's attention and uses them at the right moment.
🏃
Follow me
Snugbi leads your dog around the room at their pace. Adjusts speed to energy level. Treat at the end.
🔍
Find me
Snugbi hides, plays your voice, waits to be found. Uses their natural scent-tracking instinct.
🎓
Training mode
Record a command in the app. Camera detects compliance. Treat dispensed if correct. Train while you're at work.
🎾
Fetch simulation
Snugbi moves away fast. Dog chases. Treat on return. Simulates the fetch loop without a human present.
🗣️
Voice playback
Your recorded voice plays to soothe your dog. Activates automatically when anxiety signals are detected.
🍖
Throw treat
Spring-loaded catapult launches a treat 30–50cm. Dog chases it. Combines movement and reward.
🏃
Chase me
Snugbi moves away just fast enough to trigger the chase instinct without frustrating the pet.
🧩
Puzzle treat
Pet must interact in a specific way to earn a treat. Proven to increase activity in sedentary pets by 18%.
🔄
Novelty rotation
AI varies play patterns every 5–7 days to prevent habituation. Your pet never gets bored of Snugbi.
🌅
Morning wake up
Gentle sounds and movement at a scheduled time. Eases your pet into the day before you leave.
🌙
Wind down
Calm movement and soothing sounds before sleep time. Helps anxious pets settle at night.
👥
Two-pet mode
Separate collar IDs. Snugbi alternates attention. Separate health profiles and daily reports per pet.
